Meemo Wellness — Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Meemo Wellness ("Meemo", "we", "us") operates the Meemo skin quiz at skinmeemowellnessai.com and the Meemo text-based skincare assistant (the "SMS Bot"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact information: your mobile phone number when you text the SMS Bot, and your name or email if you choose to save a profile in the quiz.
  • Conversation and quiz content: your messages to the SMS Bot, your quiz answers (skin type, sensitivity, concerns, age range, climate, lifestyle), and any notes you add.
  • Photos: if you choose to send a selfie for skin analysis (in the quiz or by MMS), we process that image to generate your analysis.
  • Technical data: basic delivery and usage logs from our hosting and messaging providers.

How we use it

  • To run your skincare consult and generate your personalized routine.
  • To continue the conversation: answer follow-ups, remember your profile and routine, adjust recommendations, and send a small number of check-in messages after your consult (around day 3, day 14, and day 30).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.

Meemo provides cosmetic guidance only. It is not medical advice, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Text messaging terms

  • You opt in by texting the Meemo number first; the conversation is user-initiated.
  • Message frequency varies with the conversation; check-ins are limited to about three messages after a consult.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier plan.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Mobile information — including your phone number and text-message data — will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We use service providers only to run the service: Twilio (message delivery), Anthropic (AI processing of your messages, quiz answers, and any photo you send), and Vercel (hosting). Selfie images are processed by our AI provider for analysis and are not stored by Meemo. Quiz profiles saved in the web app are stored locally in your own browser.

Retention and your choices

We keep SMS conversation records for as long as needed to provide the ongoing service. You may ask us to delete your information, or simply reply STOP to end messages. To request deletion or ask a question about this policy, contact us through meemowellness.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
